How it works

    
    
      
  1. Paste a YouTube link — a video, a stream replay (VOD), or a Short.
    2. 
      
  2. AI finds the moments — Gemini picks the strongest 15–60 second moments, with a hook for the first 2 seconds and a ready caption.
    3. 
      
  3. Copy the cut commands — each moment gives you two Windows commands: one downloads that exact time range, one crops it to vertical 9:16 (720×1280).
    4. 
      
  4. Run them on your laptop — paste the commands into Command Prompt or PowerShell. You need yt-dlp and ffmpeg installed once.
    5. 
      
  5. Post the clips — add trending audio in the Instagram or YouTube app and post. Add the caption and hashtags from the card.
    6. 
    

    
Honest note: a live stream can only be clipped after it ends (from the VOD), not while it is live.
